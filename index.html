wickly

Judge the chart, not the ticker.

Wickly shows you live candlestick charts with the name hidden. Swipe right if a chart looks bullish, left if it looks bearish. Call all six timeframes of a stock and Wickly reveals which stock it was and how you called it.

Privacy

Wickly has no accounts, and your calls and verdicts stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Wickly is for practice and fun. It isn't investment advice.
