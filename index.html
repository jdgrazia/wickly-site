wickly

Judge the chart, not the ticker.

Wickly shows you live candlestick charts with the name hidden. Swipe right if a chart looks bullish, left if it looks bearish. Call all six timeframes of a stock and Wickly reveals which stock it was and how you called it.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email altjdgrazia@gmail.com. In the app, Settings → Report a problem sends us the details we need to fix it.

To report a card someone posted, tap the ⋮ on the card and choose Report this card. To report a player's photo or bio, open their profile, tap the ⋮ and choose Report. Or email us. Reports are looked at within a day, and cards, photos, bios or players that break the rules are removed.

Card rules

Cards players post go to their followers, profiles are open to everyone, and cards, profile photos and bios keep to the same rules:

  • No hate, slurs, bullying or harassment.
  • No nudity or sexual content.
  • No violence, threats or anything illegal.
  • No spam, scams or ads.
  • No one else's private information, or photos of people who didn't say yes.

Cards, photos, bios and players that break them are removed. Players agree to these rules before posting their first card.

Privacy

Wickly has no accounts, and your calls stay on your phone. If you pick a leaderboard name, your profile shows your last 12 verdicts and the photo and bio you add. Read the privacy policy.

Wickly is for practice and fun. It isn't investment advice.
