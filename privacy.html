Wickly Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Wickly is made by jdgrazia ("we"). This policy explains what the Wickly app collects and why. Questions: altjdgrazia@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Wickly has no accounts. We never ask for your real name, email or phone number.
  • Your watchlist, calls, verdicts and settings stay on your phone.
  • Each call you make adds one to an anonymous tally that can't be traced back to you.
  • If you join the leaderboard, the name you pick and your accuracy are shown to everyone.
  • If you follow friends or post cards, our server stores who you follow, your cards (with any picture you add) and your answers. Your followers see your cards (or just the one person you send a card to), and you see who answered yours.
  • If something goes wrong, the app sends us a report with technical details about your phone and the error.
  • On the Free plan, Google shows ads and, on Android, may use your device's advertising ID to do so.

What we collect

Anonymous call tallies

When you call a chart bullish or bearish, the app adds one to a count on our server for that asset, timeframe and hour. The count records whether the call was bullish or bearish and whether it was made before you knew what the asset was. Nothing else goes with it: no name, account, device ID, advertising ID or location, so the tallies can't be linked to you. To count each person once per chart, your phone remembers your own latest call and moves it if you call the same chart again.

We use the tallies to show how other people called a chart (part of Wickly Premium) and to improve Wickly. We may also share or license them in aggregate, for example as market sentiment data.

Leaderboard (only if you join)

The leaderboard is optional. When you join, you pick a name to show on it (it doesn't need to be your real name), and the app sends us that name, a random player ID it makes for you, and how many of your calls from the last 30 days have been judged and how many were right. It updates those numbers as your calls are judged. Everyone using Wickly can see your name and accuracy on the leaderboard; the player ID stays private. You can change your name or leave at any time in the app, and leaving deletes your entry. Players who don't join send none of this.

Friends and cards (only if you use them)

With a leaderboard name, you can follow other players by their names, and they can follow you by yours. Our server stores who follows whom and who you've blocked. Players who follow each other are friends, and the Friends board shows you and your friends' names and accuracy.

You can post cards to your followers: a question, and a picture or screenshot if you add one. The app only gets the picture you pick, and before sending it, it makes it smaller and removes the details photos carry, such as where and when they were taken and with what phone. Everyone who follows you sees your cards in their deck. Anyone who knows your name can follow you unless you block them; blocking also stops them seeing your cards. When your followers answer a card yes or no, you see who said what. When you answer someone's card, they see your answer, and you see how many said each. You can also send a card to just one person who follows you, like a message, and only they see it. Sending a verdict to friends posts its share card as a card's picture. Cards and their answers are deleted 7 days after they're posted, or sooner if you delete them.

You can report a card that breaks the rules, including one sent just to you. A report sends us the card's question, its author's name and the reason you pick, and we keep it only as long as we need to look into it. A card several players report is hidden until we've looked at it, and we remove cards and players that break the rules.

Leaving the leaderboard deletes your follows, blocks, cards and answers along with your entry.

Problem and crash reports

When you send a report from Settings, it includes what you write, the email address you add (if any), and details that help us fix the problem: your phone's make and model, its Android or iOS version, Wickly's version, screen and font size, language, your plan, whether notifications are on, and the app's recent log (its own technical messages, which can mention charts you looked at). If Wickly crashes or runs into an error, it sends a report with the error and the same details automatically, without a message or email. The screen where you send a report shows exactly what goes with it. We use reports only to find and fix problems.

Subscriptions

Wickly Plus and Wickly Premium are sold through Google Play on Android and the App Store on iPhone. Google or Apple handles payment, so we never see your payment details. The app checks with the store whether you have an active subscription.

Ads

On the Free plan, ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address and how you interact with ads. Learn how Google uses this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. On iPhone, Wickly doesn't ask for permission to track you, so its ads don't use your advertising identifier. Wickly Plus and Wickly Premium have no ads.

Market data

Charts come from Yahoo Finance and Polymarket. The app asks them for prices directly, so they receive standard request information such as your IP address. We don't send them anything else about you.

Server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider (Supabase) keeps short-lived request logs, which include IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting. They are not added to the tallies.

What stays on your phone

Your watchlist, calls, verdicts, settings, your own latest call on each chart and the record of which calls were right are stored only on your device, and uninstalling Wickly deletes them. The app judges your calls on your phone, using the same market data as the charts. Verdict notifications are created on your phone; we don't send them.

When you share a verdict, the app draws the image on your phone and hands it to the app you pick, or saves it to your photos if you tap the save button. We don't receive it.

Links to other services

Trade buttons open Robinhood or Coinbase. Some links include affiliate tracking, which tells that service the visit came from Wickly. Their own privacy policies cover what happens there.

Children

Wickly is for adults. It isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
